I am a postdoctoral research fellow in the group of [Prof. Hannah Price](https://hannahmprice.com//) at the [Theoretical Physics Divison](https://more.bham.ac.uk/theoretical-physics/) of the University of Birmingham. 

My broad research interests lie in the study of quantum many-body theory of ultracold atoms and molecules. Currently, my focus is on exploring the effects of frustrated geometry in strongly interacting dipolar matter with large local Hilbert spaces.
